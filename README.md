One line, at most 200 characters, about a small good thing that happened today. The warmest, most surprising line takes the $5.

## The line

> At 6 a.m. the warung lady slipped an extra fried egg onto my rice and whispered, 'for the boy who always says thank you' — she still doesn't know my name.

A small kindness from a stranger, noticed before it was ever asked for. The warmth is in the egg she did not have to give; the surprise is that she was keeping score of something as small as a thank-you, while she never even learned his name.

## Why this line

The judge weighs warmth first, then surprise, then one concrete detail, then fresh wording. This line tries to earn each one honestly:

- **Warmth:** a stranger gives something extra for no reason at all, and means it.
- **Surprise:** the reward is for politeness, not for a purchase — and she gives it to a customer whose name she does not know.
- **Concreteness:** 6 a.m., a warung, one fried egg, a whispered sentence you can almost hear.
- **Fresh wording:** no greeting-card phrasing, just a small morning scene told the way it happened.

## How it works

`main.py` is pure Python standard library. It writes the line above to `entry.txt` inside the directory named by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `output`), with a trailing newline and mode `0644`. The output is deterministic: every run writes the exact same bytes.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```
